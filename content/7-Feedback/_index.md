# 7. Reflections & Feedback on the FCAJ Program — AWS Vietnam

**Student:** Do Tran Minh Phuc  
**University:** VNU-HCM University of Science (HCMUS)  
**Internship Organization:** Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC (Bitexco Office, HCMC)

---

### 1. Overall Impressions of the FCAJ Internship Program
The 12-week internship at the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) — AWS Vietnam** program provided valuable practical exposure to cloud computing and Generative AI within an enterprise environment. Working at the Bitexco office and implementing the hands-on project (EduMedia AI) helped reinforce my foundational knowledge of cloud infrastructure, improve technical research capabilities, and deepen my understanding of Serverless architectures with AWS SAM, cost optimization, and operational best practices.

---

### 2. Satisfaction Level
* **Overall Rating:** **Highly Satisfied (5/5 Stars)**.
* **Environment & Facilities:** The Bitexco office is modern, well-equipped, and quiet with stable Internet connectivity, providing an excellent setting for study, research, and project development.
* **Program Support:** The program administration provided timely support regarding administrative procedures, attendance management, and provisioning AWS practice accounts throughout the project lifecycle.
* **Program Content:** The curriculum provided structured orientation on AWS cloud services, practical project development, and opportunities to participate in major technology events (AWS Community Day, GenAI Hackathon), broadening real-world cloud industry perspectives.

---

### 3. Areas for Further Optimization in Future Cohorts
Overall, the FCAJ program is exceptionally well-structured and comprehensive, providing excellent office facilities at Bitexco alongside active participation in premier AWS community events. To assist future student cohorts in accelerating project onboarding and technical execution, I offer a couple of constructive observations:
1. **Early Reference Architecture Guidelines:** Providing a curated set of AWS Well-Architected reference blueprints for common project patterns (such as Serverless event-driven architectures, RAG pipelines, or media ingestion workflows) during the initial planning weeks would help students scope their projects faster and streamline early service selection.
2. **Peer-to-Peer Technical Exchange:** Encouraging informal internal knowledge-sharing sessions (such as brief lightning talks or technical demos) among interns in the cohort to discuss real-world debugging experiences and cloud cost optimization (FinOps) best practices within sandbox constraints, enabling students to learn diverse approaches from their peers' projects.

---

### 4. Would You Recommend the Program to Friends? Why?
* **Answer:** **Yes**.
* **Reasons:**
  * **Bridging Theory and Industry Practice:** The program provides a structured hands-on environment that reinforces university computer science fundamentals through end-to-end cloud product development.
  * **Enterprise Work Environment:** Experience working in a professional corporate office setting, fostering discipline and enabling active participation in broader AWS tech community events.
